# Ricardo Grisales Ramírez

Journalist and researcher. I study what language does in public: what it promises, what it hides and who it leaves out.

- **IRA, Affective Resonance Index** ([ira-index.vercel.app](https://ira-index.vercel.app)): measures whether political language brings people closer or polarizes them, on a 0 to 10 scale. It grew out of my undergraduate thesis at Complutense University of Madrid (2024).
- **[oranrick.com](https://oranrick.com)**: essays, criticism and research on political language, in Spanish and English.
